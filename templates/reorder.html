How Reorder Quantity Is Calculated

The system recommends enough stock to bring the product quantity up to twice its minimum stock level.


Formula:

Suggested Reorder Quantity = (Minimum Stock × 2) − Current Stock


Example: Minimum stock = 20 units and current stock = 5 units.

Suggested reorder = (20 × 2) − 5 = 35 units.
